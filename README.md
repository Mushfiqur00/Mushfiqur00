## 🎓 Undergraduate Thesis

### H.265 / HEVC Video Steganography

**Status:** Research in Progress

My undergraduate thesis focuses on **information hiding in H.265/HEVC compressed video**.

The research explores how secret information can be embedded into video while maintaining an appropriate balance between:

- Security
- Imperceptibility
- Embedding Capacity
- Robustness
- Computational Efficiency

> Detailed methodology, implementation, experiments, and results will be added as the research progresses.
